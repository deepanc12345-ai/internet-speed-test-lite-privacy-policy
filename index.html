Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Internet Speed Test Lite handles information when you use our Android application.

1. Information We Collect

Internet Speed Test Lite does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or password.

The application performs internet speed tests to measure network performance such as download speed, upload speed, and ping.

2. Internet Connection

The app requires an internet connection to perform speed tests. During a test, network data may be transferred between your device and the test servers.

3. Advertising

Internet Speed Test Lite may display advertisements using Google AdMob.

Google may collect information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location, and interaction information according to Google's policies.

For more information, please visit Google's Privacy Policy .

4. Local History

Speed test results may be stored locally on your device so that you can view your previous test results using the History feature.

This information is stored locally and is not intended to be transmitted to our servers.

5. Sharing Results

If you use the Share Result feature, the application opens the Android sharing system so you can choose another application through which to share your test result.

6. Data Security

We do not operate a user account system for this application. We recommend keeping your Android device secure and using the latest version of the application.

7. Children's Privacy

Internet Speed Test Lite is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services including Google Mobile Ads. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer through the information provided on the Google Play Store listing.
